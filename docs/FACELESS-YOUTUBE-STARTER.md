# Zero-spend starter: channel census (draft, queued for the owner)

Seed row: `faceless-youtube-ai` — "Faceless AI YouTube channel(s)"
(`d1/seed.sql`, status `backlog`). The seed row lists a paid-tool starting
range; this starter needs none of it. This draft only describes the
experiment; the owner presses Vet / Vet-&-starter, Start, and Win/Lose
(`docs/OWNER-ACTIONS.md` §3). No spend, no new account, no channel created,
no video uploaded, no message, post, or accepted term is needed before the
week-1 number exists.

## Hypothesis

At least one of 10 reviewed faceless niches shows two or more independent
monetization signals visible on public pages alone (a channel with visible
subscriber counts and a steady upload cadence, a video description carrying
affiliate links or a sponsor mention, a niche with three or more active
channels uploading in the last 30 days). Falsified if zero niches pass.

## Metric and the single week-1 number

Metric: passing niches (0–10) — niches passing the two-signal bar, counted
0–10. The week-1 number is that one integer (for example `2/10 niches pass`).
It is a count, not dollars:
expected revenue stays unknown — no revenue figure is named anywhere in this
starter because none has evidence behind it yet.

## $0 cost breakdown

- Tools: $0 (browser + local files already owned; no new subscription).
- Data: $0 (public channel and video pages; no paid report).
- Outreach: $0 (none sent — this starter reads only).
- Total spend: $0. Owner time is the only input (about one focused evening).

## First steps

1. List 10 candidate niches from the row's brief (one line each, with the
   search that finds its channels).
2. Fix the monetization-signal rubric before reading: what counts as one signal,
   what does not (a channel's own "six figures" claim never counts).
3. Review the 10 niches oldest-first, recording at most 3 signal links per
   niche plus its pass/fail — about 15 minutes per niche.
4. Tally the single number: how many of the 10 pass the two-signal bar.
5. Decide by the number: 1+ passing niches → the next starter scripts the
   winning niche's smallest test video; 0 passing → close this experiment
   `lost` with the tally as the result and a one-line post-mortem, and Kill or
   re-scope the row's faceless angle the same day.

## Logging it

Seeds carry no review marker, so they never enter the Needs-review queue —
open the seed row's drawer instead and press `Log experiment` in the admin
zone. The modal opens prefilled for this seed — name the experiment
"Channel census: 10 faceless niches", paste the hypothesis above into the
hypothesis field, "passing niches (0–10)" into the metric field, and
"1+ passing niches" into the target field — then Log it, flip the drawer's
Status select to `testing` with Save + rescore, and Start it. Close with
Win/Lose only with the tally plus post-mortem in hand — never enter a $
figure without a receipt behind it.
