# Zero-spend starter: buyer-signal census (draft, queued for the owner)

Seed row: `digital-products-prompts` — "Prompt packs + AI templates"
(`d1/seed.sql`, capital `$0`, status `backlog`). This draft only describes the
experiment; the owner presses Vet / Vet-&-starter, Start, and Win/Lose
(`docs/OWNER-ACTIONS.md` §3). No spend, no new account, no message, post, or
accepted term is needed before the week-1 number exists.

## Hypothesis

At least one of 10 reviewed prompt-pack niches shows two or more independent
paying-buyer signals visible on public pages alone (a marketplace listing with
visible sales or reviews, a public "I paid for X" post, a priced competitor
with a changelog younger than 90 days). Falsified if zero niches pass.

## Metric and the single week-1 number

Metric: niches passing the two-signal bar, counted 0–10. The week-1 number is
that one integer (for example `3/10 niches pass`). It is a count, not dollars:
expected revenue stays unknown — no revenue figure is named anywhere in this
starter because none has evidence behind it yet.

## $0 cost breakdown

- Tools: $0 (browser + local files already owned; no new subscription).
- Data: $0 (public marketplace and forum pages; no paid report).
- Outreach: $0 (none sent — this starter reads only).
- Total spend: $0. Owner time is the only input (about one focused evening).

## First steps

1. List 10 candidate niches from the row's brief (one line each, with the
   search that finds its buyers).
2. Fix the buyer-signal rubric before reading: what counts as one signal, what
   does not (own marketing copy never counts).
3. Review the 10 niches oldest-first, recording at most 3 signal links per
   niche plus its pass/fail — about 15 minutes per niche.
4. Tally the single number: how many of the 10 pass the two-signal bar.
5. Decide by the number: 1+ passing niches → the next starter packages the
   winning niche's smallest sellable pack; 0 passing → close this experiment
   `lost` with the tally as the result and a one-line post-mortem, and Kill or
   re-scope the row's prompt-pack angle the same day.

## Logging it

Press `Vet & log starter` on the seed row, name the experiment
"Buyer-signal census: 10 prompt niches", paste the hypothesis above into the
hypothesis field and "passing niches (0–10)" into the metric field, then Start
it. Close with Win/Lose only with the tally plus post-mortem in hand — never
enter a $ figure without a receipt behind it.
